    A QUICK SOUND CHECK

    Different devices.
    Different ways to cast.

    Computers → this room

    Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Play music in a tab, click Cast a music tab, choose that tab, and enable Share tab audio. Only audio is transmitted; screen video is never sent.

    Phones → speakers

    Phones can listen in this room. Mobile browsers cannot normally share audio from YouTube, Spotify, or other apps. Use the Android companion to forward app audio, or native AirPlay directly to the Mac. This website does not appear in native device pickers.

    iPhone → your Mac, with AirPlay

    Enable AirPlay Receiver in macOS System Settings (search “AirPlay Receiver”), and allow anyone on the same network. On iPhone, open Control Center → audio output → your Mac. This plays directly on the Mac, outside the web room.

    Android native casting

    Requires a compatible Google Cast receiver or a separate native audio sender. A website alone cannot turn your Mac into a Chromecast. Install the BeatSync Android Wi-Fi sender (Android 10+), enter the Mac URL and room code, approve capture, then play your music app. No USB or debugging is required. Apps may block audio capture.

    HTTPS & network setup

    Localhost works for sending on this computer. To send from a different computer, run npm run setup:https, restart the server, trust the generated certificate on that computer, and open the HTTPS Wi-Fi address. See README for steps. Guest Wi-Fi isolation and firewalls can block connections.

    Capture depends on browser, service, and content protection. Some protected tracks may be silent. Playback across devices is best effort, not sample-synchronized.
